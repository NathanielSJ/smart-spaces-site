Frequently asked questions

Do I really need a dedicated changing station, or is a changing pad on a dresser enough?

A changing pad on a dresser works fine as a budget option, but a dedicated station usually adds storage underneath for diapers and wipes, plus safety rails that a flat dresser top doesn't have. If space or budget is tight, a pad with a strap on a sturdy, low dresser is a reasonable substitute.

Is a video baby monitor worth it over a basic audio-only monitor?

For most parents, yes — being able to see whether a baby is actually awake or just making noise in their sleep cuts down on unnecessary check-ins significantly. Audio-only monitors are lighter on the budget and still work fine for a nursery close to where you'll be, but video adds real value for peace of mind.

What actually needs to go in a diaper caddy?

At minimum: diapers in the size currently in use, wipes, a change of clothes, and diaper cream. Keeping it stocked and moving it to wherever the baby is at the time — living room, car, grandparents' house — is usually more useful than keeping it fixed in the nursery.
