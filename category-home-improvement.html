Frequently asked questions

Do I need an electrician to install a ceiling fan with a light kit?

Not if you're replacing an existing fixture that already has wiring in place — most ceiling fan and light combos can be swapped in using the same wiring as the old fixture. If you're adding a fixture where there wasn't one before, or the room has no existing ceiling wiring, that's a job for an electrician.

What size TV stand do I need for a 65" or larger TV?

As a general rule, look for a stand at least a few inches wider than your TV's total width on each side, both for visual balance and stability. A 71" stand comfortably fits most TVs up to 65", with room left over for a soundbar or speakers.

Do solar security cameras work in low-light or cloudy climates?

Most solar security cameras include a backup battery that charges during any daylight — even overcast days — and the battery itself is usually sized to run for several cloudy days without direct sun. Placement matters more than climate: a spot with a few hours of direct or indirect daylight is usually enough.
