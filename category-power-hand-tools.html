Frequently asked questions

What should a beginner's power tool set actually include?

At minimum, a drill/driver, a set of basic bits, and a few common accessories cover the vast majority of small household repairs — hanging shelves, assembling furniture, tightening loose fixtures. Anything beyond that (a saw, a grinder) is worth adding once a specific project calls for it, rather than buying it all up front.

Is a cordless shop vac powerful enough for real cleanup, or just light dust?

Modern cordless shop vacs in the higher wattage range handle sawdust, small debris, and even some wet spills comparably to a corded unit — the tradeoff is runtime, not suction power. For quick cleanup after a project rather than hours of continuous use, cordless is usually the better fit.

Can a cordless angle grinder handle metal cutting, or is it just for grinding?

Yes — with the right cutting disc swapped in, the same grinder handles metal cutting, rust removal, and surface grinding. The disc determines the job, not the tool itself, so one grinder with a few different discs covers most small metalwork tasks around the house.
