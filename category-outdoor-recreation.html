Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a screen house and a regular canopy tent?

A canopy tent typically just blocks sun or light rain with an open bottom and sides, while a screen house adds mesh walls that keep bugs out while still allowing airflow. If bugs are a concern at your usual camping or backyard spot, the mesh walls make a real difference.

Do outdoor porch swings need to be taken down in winter?

Weather-resistant models built for year-round outdoor use generally don't need to come down, though covering it or bringing cushions inside during extended snow or ice can extend its life. Check the specific material — some resin and treated-wood models handle winter better than others.

How long does a rechargeable camping fan actually last on a full charge?

It depends heavily on the fan speed setting and battery capacity, but a fan in the 40,000mAh range typically runs anywhere from one to several nights on lower speeds, less on high. Charging it fully before a trip and packing a small power bank as backup is a reasonable safety net for longer trips.
