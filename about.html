About Smart Spaces & Gadget Finds

This site is run by one person — me. I got into this because I genuinely like finding products that actually work, and because building something like this on the side has real appeal too. Every pick here comes from a mix of hands-on research, digging through reviews, and comparing suppliers before anything gets listed. New picks go up weekly, and if something doesn't hold up to that process, it doesn't make the cut — no filler, no padding the list just to have more items.

How picks are chosen

Every product on this site goes through the same process before it earns a spot. I start with research — looking at what a product actually claims to do versus what it's built for. From there, I dig into reviews, specifically looking for patterns: are people still happy after a few months, not just in the first week? For categories tied to my supplier work, I also compare specs and quality across multiple suppliers before settling on a recommendation. If a product doesn't hold up across that process, it doesn't get listed — even if it's popular or high-margin.
