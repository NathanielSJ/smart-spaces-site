About Smart Spaces & Gadget Finds

This site is run by one person, and it started from a simple frustration: most "best of" lists online are written by people who've never touched the products they're recommending. I wanted something different — a shelf of picks I'd actually stand behind, built around products that hold up once the novelty wears off.

New picks go up weekly. If something doesn't earn its spot, it doesn't make the list — no filler, no padding just to round out a category.

How picks are chosen

Every product here goes through the same process. I start by looking at what it actually claims to do, then dig into real buyer reviews — specifically looking for whether people are still happy with it months later, not just in the excitement of the first week. Anything that doesn't hold up to that scrutiny gets left off, even if it's popular or would otherwise be an easy pick.
