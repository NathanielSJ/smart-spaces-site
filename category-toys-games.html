Frequently asked questions

What age is a kids tablet actually appropriate for?

Most kids tablets in this category are designed for roughly ages 3 to 12, with parental controls that scale up as kids get older — content filters and time limits for younger kids, more open access as they get older. Check the specific age range and content library before buying, since it varies by model.

Do kids tablets need a separate case, or is the built-in protection enough?

Tablets marketed specifically as "kids" models, like the ones featured here, typically include a reinforced case as part of the design rather than as an add-on — so a separate case usually isn't necessary. If buying a standard (non-kids) tablet for a child, a heavy-duty case is worth adding separately.

How much film does an instant print camera use, and is it expensive to keep stocked?

Instant print cameras typically use small paper cartridges that print dozens of photos per pack, and refills are inexpensive compared to the camera itself. It's worth budgeting for refill paper as an ongoing cost, similar to how you'd think about ink for a printer.
