What to Pack in Your Baby's Daycare Bag

A simple, parent-friendly checklist for a happy, comfortable day at daycare. Use it the night before, and adjust it to your daycare's rules and your baby's age. Looking for more nursery gear? See our Baby & Nursery picks.

The daycare bag checklist

Diapering essentials

  • 6-8 diapers (more for a full day or a younger baby)
  • Baby wipes
  • Diaper cream
  • Diaper disposal bags

Feeding supplies

  • Bottles or breast milk
  • Formula and snacks, if your daycare allows them
  • Bibs and burp cloths
  • Sippy cup and spoon, if your baby is ready for them

Extra clothes

  • 2-3 full sets of clothes
  • Socks and underwear (or extra onesies)
  • Extra bibs
  • A weather-appropriate jacket

Nap time

  • A wearable sleep sack or small blanket, if your daycare permits it
  • Fitted crib sheet, only if your daycare asks you to bring one
  • Pacifier or lovey, if allowed

Comfort items

  • A favorite toy or lovey, if allowed
  • An extra pacifier
  • A small blanket for comfort during the day

Health and safety

  • Medication, if needed (with the paperwork your daycare requires)
  • Hand sanitizer
  • Sunscreen or lotion, if your daycare and your baby's age allow it
  • Thermometer, if your daycare asks parents to supply one

Extras

  • A wet bag for soiled clothes
  • Extra plastic bags
  • A travel-size changing pad
  • Parent contact info and emergency contacts

Quick tips for parents

  • Label everything. Use waterproof labels on bottles, clothes, and gear so nothing goes home with another child.
  • Check and restock daily. Refill diapers, wipes, and clothes every evening so the bag is never short.
  • Keep extra supplies at daycare. A spare outfit and a few diapers cover surprise accidents and late pickups.
  • Pack the night before. It saves time and stress on busy mornings.

Check your daycare's rules first. Policies on blankets, loveys, medications, sunscreen, and outside food vary, and safe-sleep guidelines for infants usually mean no loose blankets or stuffed toys in the crib. When in doubt, ask your provider.

Frequently asked questions

How many diapers should I pack for daycare?

Most parents pack 6-8 diapers for a full day, and more for newborns who change more often. Many daycares will tell you how many they expect, so ask when you enroll.

How many extra outfits does a baby need at daycare?

Two to three full sets is a common starting point, since spit-up, diaper leaks, and messy meals can all lead to more than one change in a day.

Can I send a blanket or stuffed animal for nap time?

It depends on your daycare and your baby's age. Safe-sleep guidance for infants generally advises against loose blankets and stuffed toys in the crib, so many providers prefer a wearable sleep sack. Ask your daycare what they allow.

Do I need to send medication or sunscreen?

Only if you or your child's doctor say it's needed. Most daycares require written permission and original labeled containers for any medication, and rules for sunscreen vary with your baby's age, so check with your provider and pediatrician.
