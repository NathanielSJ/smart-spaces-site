Frequently asked questions

Does outdoor patio furniture need to be covered or stored in winter?

Weather-resistant materials like treated wicker, aluminum, and powder-coated steel can generally stay outside year-round, but a breathable cover during heavy snow or extended freezing helps extend the finish's life. Cushions and fabric components are the exception — those are worth bringing inside or storing dry.

What's the actual difference between weather-resistant and waterproof outdoor furniture?

Weather-resistant materials are built to hold up to rain, sun, and humidity over time without warping or fading significantly, but they're not necessarily sealed against standing water. Waterproof is a stronger claim, usually reserved for cushions or covers with a sealed coating — worth checking which term a listing actually uses before assuming full water protection.

How many people does a 7-piece outdoor dining set actually seat?

Typically six chairs plus the table, though "7-piece" can vary by brand — always worth checking the specific seating count in the listing rather than assuming from the piece count alone, since some sets count a bench or umbrella as one of the seven pieces.
